Adelaide duo

Lemonade Fuzz

Two People. Full Band Sound.

Lemonade Fuzz is the cover act for clients who don’t want the music sitting politely in the corner — they want a room that sounds and feels like a live record.

Two voices, a full-band rig, and an obsession with getting every room right. We’re both audio engineers, so the room gets assessed and dialled in before anyone walks through the door. That part isn’t glamorous. It’s the reason the night sounds the way it does.

Honestly, we just want to play. More shows means more of that.

The live setup

Live, Not Canned

Live looping, drum machine, bass, two vocals, and triggers we control on the night.

The triggered parts are exactly that — triggered, by us, in the moment. Nothing runs on autopilot. The setlist is MIDI-clocked and sequenced like a DJ set, so transitions land tight and the energy holds across the night instead of sagging between songs.

Two people also means an easier load-in and a smaller footprint. That’s a logistics advantage for you, not a compromise on the show.

  • RigLive looping, drum machine, bass
  • TriggersLive-controlled, MIDI-clocked
  • SetUp to 120 minutes
  • SoundRoom assessed and dialled in on the day
